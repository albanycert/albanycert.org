updated: 2026-09-12
description: "The Albany CERT Block Captain program."
---

Block Captains are volunteer neighborhood leaders who coordinate preparedness activities on
their block — resident inventories, home-preparation discussions, emergency response
planning, and coordination with CERT resources.

To register as a Block Captain, email
[AlbanyCERT@gmail.com](mailto:AlbanyCERT@gmail.com) with your contact information and
CERT credentials.

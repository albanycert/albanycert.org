Block Captains are volunteer neighborhood leaders who coordinate preparedness activities on
their block — building resident rosters, discussing home preparation, and connecting
neighbors to CERT resources so a block can organize itself in a disaster.

## What a Block Captain does

As proposed by Albany CERT's Vice President, a Block Captain is asked to do just two
things:

1. **Attend the quarterly Block Captain meeting.**
2. **Be prepared to organize a roster for your block in a disaster.**

**CERT training is not required to be a Block Captain.** No door-knocking quota, and you
choose how much of your contact information, if any, is shared with your neighbors — you're
always reachable by CERT and the Albany Fire Department.

## Coverage

Albany has 190 residential blocks. Block Captains currently cover a meaningful share of
them, with the most active gaps in apartment and condo buildings rather than any single
neighborhood.

The City of Albany has a revision of the Block Captain Manual available on the City
website — it will prepare you for a disaster and help you organize your own home and
family for such an event.

## How to sign up

To confirm you are a Block Captain, email
[AlbanyCERT@gmail.com](mailto:AlbanyCERT@gmail.com) with the following information:

- Name
- Street address
- Block you are organizing
- Cell phone number
- Email address
- CERT Trained? Y/N
- GMRS call name if registered

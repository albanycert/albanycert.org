Eight free Thursday evening classes, 6–9pm, at the Albany Community Center: September 10,
17, 24, October 1, 8, 15, 22 (no class October 29), then a Skills Training day and final
exam on **November 5** at the Albany Fire Department.

**The class has started, and at this time we don't have any further openings.** If you'd
like to be on the wait list, email [AlbanyCERT@gmail.com](mailto:AlbanyCERT@gmail.com) —
when we plan our next training, wait-list members get an early heads-up and the first
opportunity to sign up, before it's announced publicly.

CERT training is free; the City of Albany and Albany Fire Department cover training costs
and each graduate's CERT backpack and gear.

## What you'll learn

The FEMA CERT Basic curriculum: disaster preparedness, fire safety, medical operations,
light search and rescue, team organization, disaster psychology, and terrorism awareness —
plus a hands-on skills day and final exam. Graduates are prepared to help organize their
neighbors and support professional responders in the first hours after a disaster.

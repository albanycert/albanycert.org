## Body text, in order

### Get Disaster Ready
**Free Disaster Ready Guide** — Protect Your Family. Be Prepared. Stay Safe.

Albany CERT is providing free disaster preparedness guides to every household in Albany.
This guide offers step-by-step instructions, emergency checklists, and worksheets to
help you and your family prepare for earthquakes, fires, power outages, and other
emergencies.

**Why This Matters for Albany** — Our city is located in an active earthquake zone, and
recent years have reminded us of the risks from wildfires and power shutoffs. Having a
plan and supplies ready can make all the difference when disaster strikes.

**What You'll Learn** — The Listos California Disaster Ready Guide helps you:
- Create a family emergency plan - Know what to do and where to meet
- Gather essential supplies - Build an emergency kit that can sustain your family
- Secure your space - Prevent injuries from falling objects
- Practice your response - Learn "Drop, Cover, and Hold On" and other life-saving actions
- Protect important documents - Minimize financial hardship after disaster

**Take Action Today** — We encourage you to:
- Read through the guide
- Make a detailed family emergency plan
- Gather supplies
- Practice your plan regularly

**Available in 16 Languages** — Guides are available in multiple languages to serve our
diverse community: English, 中文, Español, 日本語, 한국어, हिंदी, Tiếng Việt, العربية,
فارسی, Русский, Filipino, Հայերեն, دری, Hmoob, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, Українська. Language-specific
guides will be linked here soon. For now, contact us to request a guide in your
language.

**About Albany CERT** — Albany CERT (Community Emergency Response Team) is a volunteer
group dedicated to helping neighbors prepare for and respond to disasters. CERT is a
program across the United States that trains community members in basic disaster
response skills. We invite you to learn more about upcoming training opportunities and
how you can get involved in making our community more resilient.

**Get Involved** — Learn more: albanycert.groups.io. Email us: albanycert@gmail.com.
Website: www.albanycert.org.

This project is supported by the California Governor's Office of Emergency Services (Cal
OES) Listos California program. Albany CERT, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization
supporting emergency preparedness in Albany, California.

Thank you for taking steps to prepare yourself, your family, and our community. Stay
safe, stay prepared!
